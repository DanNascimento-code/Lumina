# Privacy and Data Notice — Lumina

**Document:** PRI-001 · **Version:** 5.0 · **Effective:** August 1, 2026 · **Owner:** Data Protection Office

## Data we process

Lumina processes account details, delivery addresses, order history, technical browsing events and assistant conversations to fulfill purchases, prevent fraud, provide support and improve customer experience. Full card details are processed by the payment provider and are never stored by Lumina.

## Lumi assistant

Messages may be analyzed by language models and knowledge-retrieval systems. Lumi does not need passwords, full identity documents, complete card numbers or medical information. Customers should avoid entering sensitive data. Responses cite sources when they rely on policies or product specifications.

## Retention and rights

Orders and tax records are retained for legally required periods. Support conversations are retained for up to 180 days for quality and security, with restricted access and audit logs. Data subjects may request confirmation, access, correction, portability or deletion where applicable through the privacy channel.

## Automated decisions and human support

Lumi does not make final decisions about fraud, warranty coverage or consumer rights. Higher-impact cases receive human review. Customers may request a human support specialist at any time.
