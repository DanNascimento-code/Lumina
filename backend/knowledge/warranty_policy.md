# Warranty and Technical Support — Lumina

**Document:** TEC-007 · **Version:** 2.5 · **Effective:** July 10, 2026 · **Owner:** Product Quality

## Coverage

Every Lumina product includes a 12-month contractual warranty from the invoice date, including the applicable statutory warranty period. Coverage includes defects in materials and manufacturing, batteries operating outside design parameters and electronic component failures under normal use.

## Technical support

Support begins with safe, reversible remote diagnostics. When an inspection is required, Lumina provides free return shipping. The technical center completes its initial assessment within 5 business days of arrival and proceeds with repair, replacement or another remedy according to applicable law and parts availability.

## Batteries and normal wear

Rechargeable batteries wear naturally. The warranty covers abnormal capacity loss confirmed by diagnostics, not the expected gradual reduction caused by charge cycles. Avoid extreme heat and store devices at a partial charge when unused for extended periods.

## Exclusions

Damage caused by impacts, oxidation, use outside the IP rating, uncertified chargers, firmware modification or unauthorized repair is not covered unless it is unrelated to the reported defect.

## Data and repair privacy

Customers should back up data, remove passwords and unlink accounts before shipping a device. Lumina never requests personal passwords and cannot guarantee data preservation during repairs that require a factory reset.
