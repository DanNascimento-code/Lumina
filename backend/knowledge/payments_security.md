# Payments and Financial Security — Lumina

**Document:** FIN-003 · **Version:** 3.0 · **Effective:** September 20, 2026 · **Owner:** Finance & Risk

## Payment methods

Checkout accepts Pix and major credit cards. Card purchases may be split into up to 10 interest-free payments with a minimum installment of R$50. Pix payments must be completed within 15 minutes; after that, the code expires and no order is created.

## Approval

Pix normally confirms within seconds. Card transactions are validated by the issuer and Lumina's fraud-prevention provider; most responses are immediate, but some reviews can take up to 24 hours. Lumina never requests a password, full security code, banking token or transfer to an individual's account.

## Card data and privacy

Full card details are processed by a PCI-certified payment provider and are never stored in Lumina systems. Customers can view the card brand and last four digits in their order history. Any payment change requires fresh consent in secure checkout.

## Invoices and cancellation

The electronic invoice is emailed after approval and before dispatch. Orders that have not been invoiced can be canceled under My Orders. After invoicing, support attempts to stop fulfillment or guides the customer through refusal and return.
