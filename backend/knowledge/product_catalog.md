# Official Product Catalog — Lumina Collection 2026

**Document:** CAT-026 · **Version:** 6.4 · **Effective:** October 1, 2026 · **Owner:** Product & Merchandising

## Aurora Headphones

Aurora is an over-ear headphone with adaptive active noise cancellation, transparency mode, spatial audio and up to 42 hours of battery with ANC off or 34 hours with ANC on. It weighs 268 g, uses Bluetooth 5.3 and can switch between two devices. It is the best fit for travel, focused work and long listening sessions. It is not rated for immersion.

## Nimbus Speaker

Nimbus is a 40 W portable speaker with 360-degree audio, up to 18 hours of battery and an IP67 rating for dust and temporary fresh-water immersion under the manual's conditions. Two units can form a stereo pair. It is better suited to social settings and outdoor use than Aurora.

## Orbit Watch S2

Orbit Watch S2 has a titanium case, 1.43-inch AMOLED display, dual-band GPS, 5 ATM water resistance and up to 7 days of typical battery life. It tracks sleep, activity and heart rate for wellness purposes, but it is not a medical device and does not replace professional advice. Compatible with Android 11+ and iOS 16+.

## Halo Light Mini

Halo Light Mini is a 350-lumen portable lamp with up to 10 hours of battery at medium brightness, Wi-Fi and Matter support. It provides tunable white light and color scenes. A 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network is required for initial setup.

## Flow Keys Pro

Flow Keys Pro is a wireless low-profile ABNT2 mechanical keyboard with hot-swappable linear switches, Bluetooth pairing for three devices and up to 120 hours of battery without lighting. It includes a USB-C cable and a keycap and switch removal tool.

## Arc Charger 3-in-1

Arc Charger simultaneously charges a compatible magnetic phone, watch and earbud case. It provides up to 15 W on the primary stand and includes a 1.5 m USB-C cable. Actual power depends on the device and a 30 W USB-C PD adapter, sold separately.

## Aero Buds Pro

Aero Buds Pro are compact wireless earbuds with adaptive active noise cancellation, transparency mode, Bluetooth 5.4 and multipoint pairing. The earbuds and charging case provide up to 30 hours of combined listening, and the earbuds carry an IP54 dust and splash rating. They are a lighter alternative to Aurora for commuting, calls and exercise, while Aurora remains stronger for long sessions and maximum isolation.

## Vector Mouse S

Vector Mouse S is an ergonomic wireless mouse for productivity and creative work. It pairs with up to three devices, offers an adjustable sensor up to 8000 DPI, programmable controls and up to 90 days of typical battery life. It complements Flow Keys Pro in multi-device workspaces and supports Bluetooth or its included USB receiver.

## Sentinel Cam 2K

Sentinel Cam 2K is an indoor security camera with 2K HDR video, a 135-degree field of view, person detection and dual-band Wi-Fi. A physical shutter covers the lens and disables capture when privacy mode is enabled. Recordings can use encrypted local storage or an optional cloud plan; cloud storage is not required for live viewing.

## Prism Phone X

Prism Phone X is a 5G smartphone with 256 GB of storage, a 6.4-inch 120 Hz OLED display, a stabilized dual-camera system and a 4800 mAh battery. It supports eSIM, NFC and USB-C fast charging. A charger is not included in the box; Lumina recommends a compatible USB-C PD adapter.

## Slate Tab 11

Slate Tab 11 is a thin 11-inch tablet with a 2.5K display, 128 GB of storage, quad speakers and up to 12 hours of mixed-use battery life. Desktop mode supports keyboard and mouse workflows, and an optional pressure-sensitive pen is sold separately. It is suitable for media, study and lightweight productivity but does not replace a workstation for sustained professional rendering.

## Vertex Controller

Vertex Controller is a low-latency wireless gamepad with Hall-effect sticks, adjustable triggers and up to 30 hours of battery life. It connects through Bluetooth or the included 2.4 GHz receiver and supports PC, Android and iOS. Platform support varies by game, so customers should verify that the title accepts external controllers.

## Recommendation criteria

Choose Aurora for maximum focus, travel and long sessions, or Aero for a compact commute and exercise option. Choose Nimbus for shared listening and outdoor use. Choose Orbit for activity and discreet notifications. Choose Halo for ambience, Sentinel for indoor monitoring, Flow Keys and Vector for multi-device productivity, and Arc Charger for reducing cable clutter. Prism is the all-purpose mobile choice, Slate favors a larger canvas for study and media, and Vertex is designed for gaming across compatible screens.
