# Shipping Policy — Lumina Commerce

**Document:** LOG-002 · **Version:** 3.2 · **Effective:** August 15, 2026 · **Owner:** Operations & Customer Experience

## Coverage and delivery estimates

Lumina ships to every state in Brazil. The estimate shown at checkout starts on the first business day after payment confirmation and accounts for fulfillment, transit and product availability. Capitals in the South and Southeast normally receive orders within 2–5 business days; other locations within 4–10 business days. The estimate shown for the customer's postal code at checkout always prevails.

## Shipping methods and pricing

Orders of R$499 or more include free standard shipping. Express delivery, when available for the postal code, is priced separately even when the order qualifies for free standard shipping. Standard shipping for orders below R$499 costs R$24.90.

## Tracking

After dispatch, the customer receives a tracking link by email and can follow progress under My Orders. Statuses are: payment approved, preparing, shipped, out for delivery and delivered. Carrier events may take up to 24 hours to appear.

## Address changes and delivery attempts

Customer support can correct an address before the invoice is issued. Packages cannot be redirected after dispatch. The carrier makes up to two delivery attempts. After two failed attempts, the package returns to the distribution center and support coordinates reshipment or a refund.

## Delays and damaged packages

When an order is more than two business days past its estimate, Lumina opens an investigation with the carrier and updates the customer within 48 business hours. Customers should refuse visibly opened or damaged packages whenever possible and document the issue with photos.
