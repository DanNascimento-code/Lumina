# Returns, Exchanges and Refunds — Lumina

**Document:** CX-004 · **Version:** 4.1 · **Effective:** September 1, 2026 · **Owner:** Customer Experience

## Right of withdrawal

Online purchases may be returned within 7 calendar days from delivery. The product must include its accessories, manuals and original packaging and show no signs of misuse. Carefully opening the package to evaluate the product does not invalidate the return.

## Starting a return

Customers start a request under My Orders or with Customer Support, provide a reason and receive a prepaid return label. The package must be shipped within 5 calendar days after the label is issued. Lumina sends email updates at every stage.

## Preference exchanges

Within the 7-day window, customers may choose store credit after inspection or a refund to the original payment method. Exchanges for another color or model depend on inventory; store credit provides the fastest path to a new purchase.

## Defective products

Defects reported within 30 days of delivery receive priority triage. Lumina provides free return shipping and completes the initial assessment within 5 business days after the item reaches the technical center. This policy does not limit statutory or contractual warranty rights.

## Refund timing

After approval, Pix refunds are completed within 2 business days. Card refunds are requested within 2 business days, but the credit may appear on one of the next two statements depending on the issuer. Original shipping is refunded when the return falls under the right of withdrawal.

## Exclusions

Products damaged by drops, liquid exposure outside their protection rating, improper installation or unauthorized modification are not eligible, unless the assessment confirms that the claimed manufacturing defect is unrelated to the damage.
